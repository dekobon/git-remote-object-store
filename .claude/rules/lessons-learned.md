## Lessons Learned

Hard-won project lessons go in `docs/development/lessons_learned.md`. Keep the list small and actionable — only document lessons that are genuinely hard (cost real debugging time or caused real bugs) and important (likely to recur). Err on the side of NOT adding entries. This is not a changelog or a diary.

Use the `/lessons-learned` skill to review project activity and draft entries against the quality bar.
